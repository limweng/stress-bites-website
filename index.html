Why a bear with a cookie

Stress Bites started the way most home-baking stories do — a stressful week, a very full oven, and a batch of cookies that disappeared faster than expected among friends.

The bear on our box is basically us on any given baking day: mid-bite, unbothered, mildly covered in flour. It felt more honest than a polished bakery crest.

Everything is still mixed by hand in small batches, tested on very willing neighbours, and packed the same day it's baked.
